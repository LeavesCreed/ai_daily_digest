## 1. xAI Launches Grok 4.6: AAII 61 Matches GPT-5.6 Sol at One-Fifth the Price

### What Happened
xAI officially released Grok 4.6 on August 12. The model retains Grok 4.5's V9 base (1.5T params) with all upgrades concentrated in post-training. The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (AAII) jumped from 56 (Grok 4.5) to 61, level with GPT-5.6 Sol Max and just behind Claude Fable 5 Max (62). Pricing holds at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens — roughly one-fifth of GPT-5.6 Sol's output price.

### Why It Matters
Grok 4.6 is a clear signal on the price-intelligence Pareto frontier: matching top closed-source comprehensive intelligence at extreme cost efficiency. xAI says most improvements went into long-running agents — autonomous research, large codebase work, multi-step app building with self-verification. This extends 2026's "collapsing inference cost" theme with major implications for agentic workload economics.

### Key Technical Points
- AAII 61, tied with GPT-5.6 Sol Max; AA-Briefcase long-horizon knowledge work Elo 1577, Fable 5-tier
- Pricing unchanged: $2/$6 per 1M tokens; cache hits $0.5 (up from $0.3 on 4.5)
- 500K context window; native function calling, web search, X search, code execution
- Turn efficiency: AA-Briefcase averages ~53 turns / 0.5B input tokens vs Claude Opus 5 Max ~103 turns / 2.0B
- Live on xAI API, Grok Build, Cursor, plus OpenRouter/Vercel/Cloudflare gateways

## 2. OpenAI Debuts GPT-5.6-Cyber: Found Two Chrome V8 Zero-Days Pre-Release

### What Happened
OpenAI expanded its Daybreak cybersecurity program with GPT-5.6-Cyber, a cybersecurity-specialized model for vulnerability discovery, reverse engineering, and malware analysis. OpenAI disclosed the model helped uncover two previously unknown vulnerabilities in Chrome's JavaScript V8 engine (fixed as CVE-2026-15903) and at least 5 vulnerabilities in a major mobile OS — including a chain that could let malicious apps fully control a device.

### Why It Matters
GPT-5.6-Cyber demonstrates the practical defensive value of vertical-specialized models: verifiable high-severity vulnerability findings produced before general release. It also underscores the dual-use nature of AI cyber capability — OpenAI maintains controlled, verified access for defenders. This marks AI's shift from "analysis assistance" to operational autonomous vulnerability hunting.

### Key Technical Points
- Two Chrome V8 zero-days, researcher-validated and reported to Google, fixed as CVE-2026-15903
- At least 5 mobile OS vulnerabilities including a device-takeover chain
- Part of Daybreak program expansion; controlled access for verified defenders
- Capabilities: vulnerability discovery, code analysis, system hardening, binary inspection

## 4. Suno Inks Global Licensing Deal with BMG: AI Music Copyright Milestone

### What Happened
AI music platform Suno announced a global strategic licensing alliance with music rights giant BMG on August 12, covering BMG's recorded and publishing repertoire. The agreement ensures participating BMG artists and songwriters have their rights protected and compensated, while settling prior use of BMG recordings and publishing works. It is the first major rightsholder licensing deal with Suno since Warner Music's alliance nine months ago.

### Why It Matters
AI music has long operated in a "use first, license later" gray zone. The Suno-BMG framework offers a replicable bilateral template: opt-in participation, rights protection, and clear economics. BMG emphasized "choice is the guiding principle" — a clause design that may become a baseline for future label negotiations with generative AI platforms, potentially easing the AI music litigation storm.

### Key Technical Points
- Global strategic framework covering BMG recorded and publishing repertoire
- Opt-in mechanism: participating artists/songwriters protected and compensated
- Settles prior use of BMG works
- Paves the way for Suno's upcoming new music model
- First major rightsholder deal since Warner Music alliance

## 5. NVIDIA Unveils 800 VDC Data Center Power Architecture: The AI Factory Power Standard Battle

### What Happened
NVIDIA published its 800-volt DC (800 VDC) data center power architecture as the power infrastructure blueprint for next-gen AI factories. "800 VDC unlocks the compute performance and power density required for AI at scale," said Vladimir Troy, NVIDIA's VP of data center infrastructure. Wood Mackenzie projects $9 trillion in global AI and data infrastructure investment through 2040; NVIDIA argues facilities that resolve their power architecture first will capture that spending.

### Why It Matters
Power is becoming the hard bottleneck for AI scaling, and NVIDIA is folding power architecture into its platform narrative. The hybrid approach emphasizes backward compatibility — "land, power rights, building infrastructure" can all be preserved, letting existing facilities upgrade to higher-density compute without write-offs. Analysts expect Eaton and other power equipment makers to win first 800 VDC orders (announced 2026, shipping 2028).

### Key Technical Points
- 800 VDC architecture targets AI-scale power density requirements
- Hybrid approach preserves land, power rights, and building structures for upgrades
- Wood Mackenzie: $9T global AI/data infrastructure investment through 2040
- Evercore ISI raised Eaton target to $600+, expecting first orders announced 2026
- Complements NVIDIA's $500B third-party capital platform into a capital+power+chip loop

# Important Paper of the Day

## Long-Horizon AI Research for the Grothendieck Constant

arXiv 2608.11195 (August 11). This work demonstrates an end-to-end human-AI mathematical collaboration: an AI research system working with human mathematicians tightened bounds on the Grothendieck constant KG to 6π/11 ≤ KG ≤ π/2·log(1+√2) − 3.47×10⁻⁴, thereby determining the tenths digit of KG as 7. Notably, the two theorems emerged from a "failed upper-bound search" pivoting to a lower bound. The AI system produced insights deemed novel by domain experts. The paper details AI's strengths and weaknesses in mathematical research and conditions for AI breakthrough insights — strong evidence of AI transitioning from proof assistant to research collaborator.

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11195
