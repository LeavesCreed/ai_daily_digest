## 1. StepFun Releases Step 5 Preview: 600B MoE Targeting Agentic Work

### What Happened
On September 20-21, Chinese lab StepFun released Step 5 Preview, its new flagship model for software engineering, professional knowledge work, and finance. It uses a sparse Mixture-of-Experts architecture with 600 billion total parameters and 27 billion activated per token, plus a 1-million-token context window supporting text, image, and video input.

### Why It Matters
Step 5 Preview scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it among the top three open-source models globally and above Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) at 41. Per-task cost is roughly one-eighth of Claude Opus 5, and about 42% cheaper than Gemini 3.8 Flash — a direct push along the "Pareto frontier" of intelligence-per-dollar.

### Key Technical Points
- 600B total / 27B active MoE (about 4.5% of weights per token)
- 1M-token context with text, image, video input
- API pricing: $1.00 input / $2.70 output per 1M tokens
- Open weights scheduled for October 15, 2026
- Targets agentic software engineering, professional knowledge, finance

## 3. Big Tech's $300B Hidden AI Guarantees Surface

### What Happened
The Financial Times reported that technology companies have provided as much as $300 billion in "residual value guarantees" over the past 12 months to fund AI data centers and chips, without those obligations appearing as conventional debt on balance sheets.

### Why It Matters
Nvidia provided a $105 billion guarantee to SB Energy for an OpenAI Ohio data center campus (debt off Nvidia's books until 2028), Broadcom $29 billion for chips leased to Anthropic, and Meta $28 billion for its Louisiana data center. Nvidia's CFO said ~25% of fiscal 2028 sales will come from customers it has financed. This shifts how investors must assess AI buildout risk beyond headline capex.

### Key Technical Points
- Nvidia: $105B Ohio (OpenAI) + up to 25% of certain financing packages
- Broadcom: $29B for chips leased to Anthropic
- Meta: $28B Louisiana data center guarantee
- Nvidia total disclosed gross exposure ~$108.5B
- Morgan Stanley: off-balance-sheet commitments across seven firms larger than reported

# Important Paper of the Day

## Grounding Agent Memory

A September 2026 paper from researchers examining production agents such as GitHub Copilot identified a critical flaw in persistent memory handling: agents fail to properly ground retrieved memories against the current context, leading to stale or conflicting recall. The work proposes grounding mechanisms to bind memory retrieval to task context, directly addressing the "stateless chatbot" era's end as persistent memory becomes standard in agent infrastructure.

# Trend Observation

The frontier is splitting into two simultaneous races. On one side, model labs compress intelligence-per-dollar (Step 5 Preview at 1/8 Opus 5 cost, open weights accelerating) while on the other, the infrastructure layer is being financed through increasingly opaque off-balance-sheet guarantees that shift risk away from headline debt. The open-source agent runtime is standardizing (computer-use fleets, security-audit skills) even as the financial engineering behind compute becomes harder to price. The dominant question is no longer "who has the smartest model" but "who carries the residual value risk when the buildout stops growing."
