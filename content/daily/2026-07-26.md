## 1. Nvidia Locks Down Memory Supply from SK Hynix as Part of $500B AI Deal

### What Happened
Nvidia announced it secured AI memory supply from SK Hynix as part of a $500B+ multi-year initiative spanning large-scale AI data centers and next-generation high-bandwidth memory (HBM) development. SK Telecom affiliate will build a 2GW cloud business using Nvidia's Vera Rubin systems, targeting capacity online by 2027. Nvidia also invested $1B into Naver, a Korean cloud company.

### Why It Matters
This deal confirms memory supply as the critical bottleneck in AI infrastructure scaling. With the AI boom creating a global HBM shortage, Nvidia's aggressive lock-up of SK Hynix production ensures its GPU roadmap has guaranteed memory for Vera Rubin and future architectures. The involvement of SK Telecom as a cloud builder signals that massive AI infrastructure buildouts are moving beyond hyperscalers to foreign governments and conglomerates.

### Key Technical Points
- Agreement valued at over $500B across multiple years
- SK Telecom plans 2GW data center powered by Vera Rubin + HBM4
- Nvidia targets co-development of next-gen SK Hynix AI memory
- $1B investment into Korean cloud company Naver
- SK Hynix recently listed on Nasdaq to finance infrastructure

## 2. Samsung Wins $200B Broadcom AI Chip Partnership

### What Happened
Samsung Electronics announced a pact with Broadcom to widen cooperation across memory chips, contract chip making, and advanced packaging, valued at over $200B through 2030. The deal covers Broadcom's next-gen communications chips on sub-2nm process technology and collaboration on next-generation HBM products.

### Why It Matters
This partnership reshapes the custom AI chip landscape. Broadcom, one of the world's leading custom AI chip designers, commits long-term production to Samsung's foundry, potentially closing the gap with TSMC. As global tech companies increasingly develop their own custom AI accelerators, this deal positions Samsung as a major beneficiary of the custom silicon megacycle.

### Key Technical Points
- $200B+ partnership through 2030
- Sub-2nm process for Broadcom's next-gen communications chips
- Collaboration on next-gen HBM products
- Combines Broadcom's ASIC design expertise with Samsung's manufacturing
- Samsung seeks to narrow foundry gap with TSMC

### Links
- Official: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/25/samsung-electronics-wins-200-billion-broadcom-ai-chip-partnership.html

## 3. OpenAI Signs Open-Weights Coalition Letter as Roster Grows to 35 in 24 Hours

### What Happened
OpenAI added its name to "Open Weights and American AI Leadership" — a policy letter it was absent from at launch on July 24. The coalition grew from 25 to 35 signatories in a day, adding OpenAI, Cisco, Cohere, GitHub, DoorDash, Fireworks AI, Palo Alto Networks, Nous Research, and Prime Intellect. Anthropic and Google remain the notable holdouts.

### Why It Matters
This marks the crystallization of two identifiable blocs in AI policy: the open-weights coalition now spans cloud infrastructure, security, developer tooling, applications, and challenger labs. OpenAI's signature provides political cover for its existing gpt-oss product line while the closed-vs-open dividing line now has clean anchor points. Washington is actively weighing restrictions on downloadable and Chinese open-weight models.

### Key Technical Points
- 35 signatories and growing, up from 25 at launch
- OpenAI joined despite being a predominantly closed-frontier company
- Anthropic and Google remain absent
- Letter defends distillation as legitimate technique
- Targets Washington policymakers considering open-weight restrictions

## 4. Kimi K3 Open Weights Arrive Sunday: Largest Open-Weight AI Model Ever

### What Happened
Moonshot AI will publish full model weights for Kimi K3 — 2.8 trillion parameters, the largest open-weight AI model ever — on Hugging Face on July 27. The model uses sparse MoE with 896 experts (16 active), introduces Kimi Delta Attention for efficient 1M-token context, and scored first on Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena. However, it faces US government distillation allegations and Chinese export control uncertainty.

### Why It Matters
K3's open-weight release will be the most significant expansion of independent research access in AI history — if the geopolitical window stays open. The White House has accused Moonshot of distilling Anthropic's Fable model; Treasury has threatened sanctions. On China's side, the Ministry of Commerce is consulting on export controls. Sunday's release may represent a narrow window for unrestricted access.

### Key Technical Points
- 2.8T total parameters, sparse MoE (896 experts, 16 active per token)
- Effective compute cost of ~50B parameter dense model
- Kimi Delta Attention for 6.3x faster long-context decoding
- 1M-token context window, first on Arena.ai Frontend Code Arena
- Requires ~1.4TB GPU memory for self-hosting (64+ accelerators)
- Hallucination rate ~51%, time-to-first-token ~161s

## 5. DeepSeek Suspends $7B+ Funding Round After Founder Comments Go Viral

### What Happened
DeepSeek told prospective investors in its second fundraising round that it's suspending the deal, days after comments widely attributed to founder Liang Wenfeng about US-China AI competition went viral. The suspension stemmed in part from Liang's frustration over online reports about his investor comments. DeepSeek may still resume the deal or proceed with IPO preparations that could file as soon as this year.

### Why It Matters
This signals growing friction between Chinese AI labs and the geopolitical spotlight. DeepSeek's first round closed in June at $7B, and the pause suggests that regulatory scrutiny and public attention are creating operational complications for Chinese frontier labs. The IPO preparation indicates that public markets may become an alternative funding path for Chinese AI companies.

### Key Technical Points
- Second fundraising round paused after viral posts
- First round closed June 2026 at $7B
- IPO preparations may file as soon as this year
- Founder Liang Wenfeng's comments on US-China AI competition went viral
