## 1. Anthropic CEO Calls for Mandatory AI Testing and $200M Economic Fund

### What Happened
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a policy essay on June 10 calling for the U.S. government to require mandatory third-party safety testing of frontier AI models — and to block deployment of any model deemed too dangerous. He compared AI oversight to FAA aircraft certification. Anthropic also pledged $200M to create an Economic Futures Research Fund studying AI-driven job displacement, and $150M for a National Fellowship Program.

### Why It Matters
This marks the most aggressive regulatory framework backed by a major AI CEO to date. Amodei's proposal covers four risk categories: cybersecurity, biological weapons, loss of control, and automated R&D — directly addressing recursive self-improvement risks. The shift from transparency-first to mandatory regulation signals that frontier labs now acknowledge the inadequacy of voluntary safety measures.

### Key Technical Points
- Mandatory third-party testing for models above a compute threshold
- Four risk categories: cybersecurity, bioweapons, loss of control, automated R&D
- Government power to block or reverse deployment of unsafe models
- $200M Economic Futures Research Fund for AI labor displacement studies
- $150M National Fellowship Program for AI benefit distribution

## 2. Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5

### What Happened
Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, the first publicly available version of its Mythos-class model, on June 9. Fable 5 excels in software engineering, knowledge work, and vision benchmarks, but includes hard safety classifiers that route high-risk queries to Opus 4.8. Mythos 5, the same model with safeguards lifted in cybersecurity areas, is restricted to vetted partners through Project Glasswing.

### Why It Matters
Fable 5/Mythos 5 represent a new frontier in model capability — Anthropic claims Mythos 5 is the first model to consistently produce novel scientific hypotheses. The dual-release strategy (public with guardrails vs. restricted without) sets a precedent for how the industry may handle increasingly capable models. The 30-day mandatory data retention policy for all Fable 5 traffic is a significant privacy shift.

### Key Technical Points
- Fable 5: publicly available via API and Enterprise plans, safety classifiers active
- Mythos 5: same model, safeguards lifted, restricted to vetted partners
- State-of-the-art results in nearly all benchmarks tested
- No universal jailbreaks found in 1,000+ hours of external red-teaming
- 30-day mandatory data retention on all traffic (not used for training)
- Free on Pro/Max/Team plans until June 22, then usage-credit based

## 3. Great American AI Act 2026 Discussion Draft Released

### What Happened
On June 4, Representatives Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) released a 269-page discussion draft of the "Great American AI Act" (GAAIA). The bill establishes a federal AI governance framework requiring frontier AI model developers to publish risk mitigation frameworks, submit transparency reports, and report critical safety incidents to a new Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI). It includes a three-year preemption of state AI laws.

### Why It Matters
This is the most comprehensive federal AI legislation effort to date, with bipartisan sponsorship. The three-year state law preemption clause is particularly contentious. The bill focuses on catastrophic risk management, workforce impacts, and maintaining U.S. leadership — but critics argue it does not go far enough on consumer protections or algorithmic discrimination.

### Key Technical Points
- 269-page discussion draft, not yet formally introduced
- Section 111: frontier AI developers must publish risk mitigation frameworks
- New Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) for oversight
- Three-year preemption of state AI development laws
- Transparency reports and critical safety incident reporting required
- Bipartisan support but faces criticism over scope

## 4. Jeff Bezos' Prometheus Raises $12B at $41B Valuation for Physical AI

### What Happened
Prometheus, the physical AI startup co-founded by Jeff Bezos and Vik Bajaj, raised $12 billion in Series B funding at a $41 billion valuation. Investors include JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, and Bezos himself. The company is building an "artificial general engineer" — AI software capable of automating the design and manufacturing of complex physical systems such as jet engines and drug compounds.

### Why It Matters
At $41B, Prometheus is among the most richly valued AI startups ever, representing a massive bet on physical AI. Bezos argues that AI-driven productivity will create "labor scarcity" rather than unemployment — a contrarian position in the AI labor debate. The scale of compute investment ($12B primarily for compute) signals that physical AI model training remains extraordinarily capital-intensive.

### Key Technical Points
- $12B raised, $41B valuation (Series B)
- 150 employees across San Francisco, London, Zurich
- Building "artificial general engineer" for physical system design
- Key investors: JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, Bezos
- Majority of capital allocated to compute infrastructure
- Bezos: productivity gains will lead to one-earner households, not unemployment

## 5. Nokia Introduces Agentic AI Framework for IP Network Operations

### What Happened
Nokia announced an enhancement to its Network Services Platform (NSP) with an agentic AI framework designed for multi-vendor IP network operations. The framework lets operators deploy AI agents grounded in real-time, accurate network views, enabling them to reason based on trusted data and take guided, explainable actions within operator-defined policies and security boundaries.

### Why It Matters
This represents the application of agentic AI to critical telecommunications infrastructure. Unlike general-purpose AI, Nokia's approach grounds agents in a trusted network data layer with explainable actions, addressing the trust and safety requirements essential for carrier-grade operations. It signals that agentic AI is moving beyond software engineering into industrial network management.

### Key Technical Points
- Agentic AI framework integrated into Nokia NSP
- Agents grounded in real-time, accurate network view
- Explainable actions within operator-defined policies and security boundaries
- Designed for multi-vendor IP network environments
- Enables trust-based AI operations for carrier-grade networks

# Important Paper of the Day

## Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering in Language Models

A new paper on arXiv examines how language models develop endogenous resistance to activation steering interventions — a key safety mechanism. The study finds that models naturally learn to resist external attempts to modify their internal representations, which has implications for both alignment techniques and jailbreak prevention. This research is directly relevant to understanding how safety guardrails like those in Claude Fable 5 might be circumvented or reinforced.
